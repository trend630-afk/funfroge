About Neon Target Rush

Neon Target Rush is a free, fast-paced browser reaction game from FunFroge Games. Test your reflexes in a 30-second challenge by clicking or tapping glowing targets before time runs out. Build combos, improve your accuracy and try to beat your personal best score. No download or installation is required.

How to Play Neon Target Rush

  1. Select the Start Game button.
  2. Watch for the glowing target in the play area.
  3. Click or tap the target as quickly as you can.
  4. Keep hitting targets to build your combo.
  5. Avoid empty taps because they break your combo.
  6. Try to achieve your highest score before the 30-second timer ends.

Scoring and Combos

Each successful target hit earns 100 base points. Consecutive successful hits can increase your combo bonus, helping you build a higher score. Your results include your score, hits, accuracy and best score.

Tips to Improve Your Reaction Speed

  • Keep your attention on the play area.
  • Use accurate clicks or taps instead of rushing randomly.
  • Try to maintain consecutive hits for better combos.
  • Practise regularly and compare your results.
