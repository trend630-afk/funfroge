About Number Merge

Number Merge is a free online number puzzle game from FunFroge Games. Slide matching number tiles together on a 4×4 board to create larger numbers and increase your score. Use strategic power-ups, plan your moves and reach the 2048 milestone. Keep playing to challenge your personal best. No download is required.

How to Play Number Merge

  1. Start a new game on the 4×4 board.
  2. Use the arrow keys or W, A, S and D to move the tiles.
  3. On a touchscreen, swipe in the direction you want to move.
  4. Merge matching numbers to create larger tiles.
  5. Plan each move because every valid move adds a new tile.
  6. Reach 2048 and continue playing to improve your score.

Understanding the Power-ups

  • Shuffle: Rearranges the tiles on the board.
  • Remove tile: Removes a tile to create more space.
  • Double points: Activates a points bonus.
  • Undo: Lets you reverse your previous move.

Tips and Strategies

  • Think ahead before moving the tiles.
  • Keep your larger tiles organised in one area.
  • Avoid filling the board with disconnected numbers.
  • Use power-ups strategically when you need them.
  • Keep enough empty spaces available for new tiles.

Frequently Asked Questions

Yes. Number Merge is free to play in your web browser.

Merge matching number tiles and reach the 2048 milestone.

The game ends when no valid moves remain on the board.
