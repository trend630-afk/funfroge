Privacy Policy

This page explains what information may be handled when you visit FunFroge Games and play its browser games.

Information that may be handled

The games do not require an account or ask you to submit personal information to play. If you choose to contact us after an email address is provided, we will receive the address and message you send.

The platform hosting this website may process basic technical information needed to deliver pages, such as your IP address, browser or device type, requested page, and request time. What the host records depends on the hosting service and its settings.

Scores and local storage

The games use your browser's local storage to keep game-related information on your device, including high scores, reaction times, statistics, and achievements. This information is used to show your records when you return to a game. It is not sent to a FunFroge Games server by the game code.

You can remove this information through your browser's site-data or storage settings. Clearing it may reset saved scores and progress.

Cookies and similar technologies

The current project code does not set cookies. It uses local storage for the game information described above. If cookies or other similar technologies are introduced later, this policy will be updated to explain their purpose.

Third-party services

The current project contains no analytics, advertising, tracking, or other third-party service scripts. The hosting platform may process technical information as part of serving the website. If other third-party services are added later, this policy will be updated to describe them.

How information may be used

Information described on this page may be used to operate and secure the website, remember game records in your browser, respond to messages you send, and understand or address technical problems. The site does not currently use advertising or analytics scripts.

Children and privacy

The games can be played without creating an account or submitting personal information. Parents and guardians should guide children's use of websites and should avoid sending personal information through any contact channel. If you have a privacy concern, contact us using the details below.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be revised as the website changes. The current version will be posted on this page with its updated wording. Please review this page from time to time.
