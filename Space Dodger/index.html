About Space Dodger

Space Dodger is a free online space survival game from FunFroge Games. Pilot your spaceship through dangerous asteroid fields, avoid incoming obstacles and collect shields to protect yourself. Survive for as long as possible, earn points and challenge your best score. Play directly in your browser without downloading anything.

How to Play Space Dodger

  1. Select Start Game to begin your space survival run.
  2. Use the left and right arrow keys or A and D to move your ship.
  3. On touchscreen devices, use the LEFT and RIGHT controls.
  4. You can also drag or hold a direction to move your spaceship.
  5. Avoid asteroids and collect shields when they appear.
  6. Survive as long as possible and try to beat your best score.

Scoring, Lives and Shields

Your score tracks your progress during each run. Keep an eye on your survival time and remaining lives. Shields provide additional protection, so look out for opportunities to collect them.

Tips for a Better Survival Run

  • Watch the play field and anticipate incoming asteroids.
  • Move carefully instead of making unnecessary movements.
  • Keep track of your remaining lives.
  • Collect shields when it is safe to do so.
  • Practise regularly and aim to improve your best score.
