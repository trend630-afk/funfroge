About Memory Match

Memory Match is a free browser-based card game that challenges you to remember where symbols are hidden. Turn over two cards at a time, look for identical pairs, and clear the board. Each round combines observation, concentration, and a little planning. You can play at your own pace, choose a difficulty, and work toward a better score.

How to Play Memory Match

  1. Choose Easy, Normal, or Hard from the difficulty menu.
  2. Select one face-down card to reveal its symbol.
  3. Select a second card and compare the two symbols.
  4. If they match, the pair stays revealed. If they do not, remember their positions before they turn face-down again.
  5. Continue until every pair has been found. Try to finish in fewer moves and improve your time.

Choose Your Difficulty

Easy: A relaxed starting point with 4 pairs (8 cards). It is useful for learning the rules and practising how to remember card locations.

Normal: The standard challenge with 8 pairs (16 cards). It offers a balance between a manageable board and a stronger memory test.

Hard: The largest board, with 12 pairs (24 cards) arranged across 6 columns. More cards mean more positions to keep track of.

Tips for Finding More Pairs

  • Scan the whole board: Notice where each revealed symbol is located instead of focusing only on the two cards you just selected.
  • Build location groups: Mentally remember a few positions at a time, such as the top row or left side of the board.
  • Use every turn: When two cards do not match, take a moment to remember both symbols and their locations.
  • Stay consistent: A calm, repeatable approach can help you avoid forgetting cards you have already seen.
  • Practise on Easy first: Move up to Normal and Hard when you feel comfortable with the board.

Score, Moves, Time, and Achievements

The game displays your moves, completed matches, elapsed time, and score while you play. Your best-move record helps you track improvement on each difficulty. After a win, review your results and performance rating, then choose Play Again to start another round. The achievements area gives you additional goals as you play, including completing games and taking on the harder settings.

Your player statistics are stored in this browser, so they are associated with the browser and device you use. Clearing browser storage or switching devices may reset or separate those records.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Memory Match free to play?

Yes. Memory Match is free to play in a modern web browser. No game download is required.

How many cards are in each difficulty?

Easy has 8 cards, Normal has 16 cards, and Hard has 24 cards.

What counts as a move?

A move is one attempt made by turning over two cards. Finding a matching pair completes that pair; a non-matching attempt still counts as a move.

Can I play again?

Yes. Use Restart during a round or Play Again after completing it to begin a fresh game.

Will my statistics appear on another device?

Player records are saved locally in your browser. They do not automatically sync between different browsers or devices.
